# Superstore_sales-
I recently built an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard using the Superstore dataset, where I analyzed sales performance, profitability trends, customer segments, and product insights to support business decision-making.  



🔧 Tools Used

✔ Python (Pandas, NumPy) — data cleaning & feature engineering
✔ Power BI — data modeling, DAX & dashboard design
✔ Excel/CSV — dataset handling

🔹 Sales Trend Analysis
✔ Sales show strong peaks during Q4 months
✔ Consistent growth trend over time

🔹 Category Performance
✔ Technology generated the highest revenue
✔ Office Supplies performed well with strong margins
✔ Furniture had high sales but lower profitability

🔹 Top Product Insights
✔ A small group of products drive a major portion of revenue
✔ High-selling items are mostly in Technology & Office Supplies

🔹 Profitability & Discounts
✔ Higher discounts were linked to reduced profit margins
✔ Some Furniture items showed negative profit despite strong sales
